        Subscribe, and it stays up to date

        When UWI changes the timetable, your calendar changes too. Calendar apps check every few hours; Google can take up to a day.

        You changed your timetable since you subscribed. Subscribe again with this new link, then remove the old "UWI timetable" calendar.

        Apple Calendar Google Calendar Outlook

        For Google, copy the link first: Google opens its "From URL" page, and you paste it there.

        The link lists your courses, not your name, but your calendar provider keeps it, and anyone you give it to can see them. It is this timetable as it is now: if you change it here, subscribe again with the new link.

        Or download a file

        1. Google Calendar, on a computer (the phone app cannot import): make a new calendar called "UWI timetable", then Settings, Import & export, and import the file into it.
        2. Apple Calendar: open the file and choose a new calendar when asked.
        3. Outlook: Add calendar, then Upload from file, into a new calendar.

        The file is a copy, not a live link. When your timetable changes, delete that calendar and import a new file, so no old class is left behind.
